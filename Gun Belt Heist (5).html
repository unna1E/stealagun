GUN BELT HEIST

WASD move · Mouse look · Shift sprint · E buy / steal · T skip 60s of the clock

Walk up to a gun on the conveyor and press E to buy it for your base (blue, left front). Better rarities cost more but earn more money per second.

Your laser door only lets you in. Every 40 seconds (+10s per owner rebirth) each base’s doors unlock for 10 seconds (the only time you can steal): guard your guns, or sneak into a rival base and steal theirs.

Stand next to your own gun and press E to upgrade it (up to Lv10). Step on the green pad in your base to unlock more slots (up to 40, with a 2nd story).

Rebirth at the purple pad for x1.5 income each time. Buy items at the SHOP stall right of the belt: 1-4 equip, click to use, Space to jump. While doors are open, step on the red button in your base to seal your own door. F sells the gun next to you; R picks it up and places it again.

Arena (past the end of the belt): hold click to fire your best gun at bots and the boss, X swaps guns. A 50M-HP boss shows up every 15 min for 5 min. P = party menu (friends without accounts can join with invite codes), Enter = chat.

The roster cycles every 10 minutes. A secret gun rides the belt every 15 minutes, and an exotic every 45.
